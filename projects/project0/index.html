Photos & Frustums 📸

Playing dress-up with focal length, distance, and one very patient camera lens.

Part 1 Selfie: The Wrong Way vs. The Right Way

Take a picture of your friend (or yourself) from close up — a typical distorted selfie. Then step back, zoom in, and take more shots, trying to keep the face about the same size in every frame. If you did it right, the far-away/zoomed-in shot should look a lot more flattering than the up-close one. Think about why this is.

Part 3 The Dolly Zoom 🎬

Time to duplicate a classic film trick — the dolly zoom, a.k.a. the "Vertigo shot." Move the camera back while zooming in (or vice versa) so the subject stays the same size while the background stretches dramatically. Shoot several stills doing this, then stitch them into an animated GIF — you've got a dolly zoom!
