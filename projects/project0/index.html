Part 1 Selfie: The Wrong Way vs. The Right Way

🖊️ Why does this happen?

The close-up photo looks distorted because the camera is very close, so the nose is much closer to the camera than the rest of the face. When we step back and zoom in, the distance between different parts of the face becomes relatively smaller, making the face look more natural.

Part 2 Architectural Perspective Compression

🖊️ Why does this happen?

The first photo looks more compressed because the camera is farther away and uses zoom, which makes the different parts of the building appear closer together. In the second photo, the camera is closer, so the perspective makes the front and back parts of the building look more different in size.
